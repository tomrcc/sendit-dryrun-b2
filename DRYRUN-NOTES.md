### How the `translate-site` scripts behaved

- `prepare-content-translation.mjs` (whole `content` → `content_fr`): found 5 files, all untranslated. It walked `blog/` and extracted nested block fields by path. It skipped `_uuid`, `_name`, dates, image paths, categories, author and `link`/`url`. It also skipped the one-word real-text `title_suffix: last`, and included `$`/`+` and team member names.
- `merge-content-translation.mjs`: patched all 5 files in one pass and deleted the manifest. It accepted an extra key added to `translated_frontmatter`. It rewrote `>-` folded scalars as single-line `|-`. Nothing was refused.
- `prepare-translation.mjs`: classified all 160 keys and wrote the task with tone examples. On re-run: 135 current, 13 kept, 12 untranslated (the deliberate English-only posts).
- `merge-translation.mjs`: merged 148, sorted the file, raised no HTML warnings on `<strong>` or `&amp;` values, and wrote 13 entries to `rosey/translate-site-keep.json` ("Marketing", "Contact", "Blog", "Pro", "© Copyright", "minutes"…).

## Things to tell the user

- Home, About, the blog listing and the two French posts are edited as files in **Pages (Français)** / **Blog (Français)**, with no inline locale switcher. Everything else (Feature, Pricing, Contact, Privacy, 404, category pages, English-only posts, nav, footer, buttons) is translated inline with the RCC or in the **Locales** collection.
- To translate another post, copy it to `content_fr/blog/` with the same filename (don't change `slug`/`url`) and translate it. Until then it exists at `/fr/blog/<post>/` with an English body and French UI, and isn't listed on `/fr/blog/`.
- On Rosey-copied pages, cards for posts that have a French file show the English title.
- Set `CLOUDCANNON_SYNC_PATHS=/rosey/` in the existing site's settings (the `initial-site-settings.json` entry only applies to a new site). Commit `rosey/base.json`, `rosey/locales/fr.json` and `rosey/translate-site-keep.json`.
- Pre-existing, not changed: the footer logo links to `/home` (404 in both languages).
